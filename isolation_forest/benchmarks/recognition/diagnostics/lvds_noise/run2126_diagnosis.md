Verdict:
Weakly observable from the benchmark prompt and missing critical DAQ/trigger context. Both wrong labels are understandable from the aggregated AutoDQM morphology; Luna also made a category-level reasoning leap because the prompt contained no VMax evidence.

Evidence:
- The LLM received 65/86 anomalous subruns and 936 aggregated entries, dominated by extreme `nPulses_median`, pulse-height/area features, broad channel involvement, and an aggregated missing-channel list. It did not receive temporal ordering or the known board-matching, queue, rate-mismatch, or LVDS-channel-localization facts.
- Benchmark checks passed: target 2126 was removed, peer 2068 remained, the target ID was masked, no target ground truth leaked, Luna/Sol prompts and filtered KBs were identical, and every configured candidate was visible.

Of 86 subruns, 65 are anomalous (0.756), with 936 entries (10.88/subrun): 620 statistical, 219 statistical+IF, 80 IF-only, and 17 missing-channel rows. The first sparse anomaly is at subrun 9; counts rise from 2 at subrun 26 to 10, 11, 7, 10, 15, 15, and 23 in subruns 27-33. Anomalous-subrun fractions by run quartile are 0.143, 0.864, 1.000, and 1.000. Temporal input would therefore materially improve diagnosis.

The inspected LVDS pin fractions do not show a decisive onset. Explicit DAQ queue occupancy, unmatched-event bookkeeping, apparent digitizer-rate telemetry, and dynamic mask-test results were not found in machine-readable campaign/EOS files; those decisive facts and localization are present only in KB/eLog-derived provenance text.
- Post-hoc diagnostics show that 2126 is much closer in aggregate morphology to Luna's VMax examples than to 2068: feature/channel cosines are 1702 0.751/0.864 and 1703 0.751/0.865, versus 2068 0.539/0.390. Exact-signature overlap remains low (about 0.020), and the target prompt contains no VMax feature, so the morphology supports analogue selection but not the `vmax_distribution_shift` label itself.

Why recognition succeeded/failed:
- Luna matched the broad affected-channel set and extreme pulse-feature distribution to 1702/1703, then inferred VMax without direct VMax evidence. The analogue choice is quantitatively understandable; the causal/category conclusion is partly a model reasoning failure.
- Sol interpreted broad readout loss, extreme pulse counts, and the aggregated missing-channel list as `digitizer_lockup`, supported by DAQ cases 1620/1500/1604 and weak peer 2068. Its 0.35 confidence reflects ambiguity. The most discriminating LVDS evidence was absent, so this is mainly missing context plus a different observable LVDS mode, not retrieval failure.
- AutoDQM alone does not make `LVDS_noise` uniquely observable. The decisive signature is cross-system and temporal: normal TriggerBoard rate with collapsing Digitizer/TriggerBoard matching, queue/unmatched-event behavior, and mask-test localization.

Benchmark implication:
- Do not include 2126 in headline recognition accuracy under the current aggregated prompt. Retain it as a stress test, or restore it to headline evaluation only after adding temporal cross-system DAQ/trigger evidence.
- Runs 2068 and 2126 are plausibly related LVDS contact/connection subfamilies and share a physical inspect/reseat remedy, but their supplied AutoDQM signatures are different failure modes; one KB category should not imply one recognition family.
- Highest-value additions are Digitizer/TriggerBoard matching versus subrun/event, queue occupancy and unmatched-event fraction, rate comparison, dynamic trigger-mask results/channel mapping, and per-subrun feature/missing-channel evolution.
